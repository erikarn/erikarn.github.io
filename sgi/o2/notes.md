## Repair / Replacement Notes

### CD-ROM ejection failure / cracked gear

The Toshiba CD-ROM will eventually break a nylon gear in the ejection
mechanism making the unit fail to eject/retract the tray.

The problem is a small nylon cog which cracks over time.

The replacement cog has the following dimensions:

 * in "cog" speak - "0.4M 12 Teeth 2A"
 * the brass replacements on ebay may need the hole bored out a litle bit to
   fit the motor shaft
 * Total height (Teeth + shaft height): 5mm
 * Teeth height: 3mm
 * Shaft height: 2mm
 * Teeth diameter: 5.4mm
 * Shaft diameter: 3.6mm
