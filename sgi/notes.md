# Silicon Graphics Hardware / Software

Silicon Graphics has a special place in my heart. Seeing the SGI Indy
do what it was doing - with stereo shutter glasses as well! - way back
in 1995 likely cemented my trajectory into software engineering.

## Overview

I'm trying to collate the random bits and pieces that exist out there
on the web in general. There's a lot of PDFs spread all over the place.
